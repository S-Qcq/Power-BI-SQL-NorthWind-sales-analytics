# Power-BI-SQL-NorthWind-sales-analytics
A dashboard built using sales and logistics data from a fictional company (NorthWind), made available by Microsoft for relational database and management system tutorials.

What it tracks:

- Monthly sales KPIs — total revenue, total quantity sold, number of orders, and average order value, each with a Month-over-Month comparison to highlight trends
- Product performance — top products by revenue for the selected month, with MoM growth indicators to identify which categories and products are gaining or losing ground
- Employee overview — employee performance in terms of the number of orders processed (on time and delayed) and revenue generated from the sales they handled
- Customer overview — who the customers are, how much they spend, and where they come from
- Logistics & shipping — On Time Delivery (OTD) rate, average days to ship, and late orders, broken down by shipping company with MoM trends
